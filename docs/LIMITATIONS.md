# Limitations

- Results are aggregated to agro-climatic strata and therefore do not capture all local heterogeneity.
- The study identifies statistical association and consistency, not a single proven causal mechanism.
- Dynamic World land-cover change (2016–2025) does not cover the full vegetation-trend period (2001–2025).
- Hansen forest loss identifies tree-cover loss but does not independently establish its cause.
- Conflict attribution is deferred because the available conflict source was not spatially representative of Northern Nigeria.
- The 81-scenario robustness analysis tests plausible threshold combinations but is not an exhaustive model-uncertainty analysis.
- The old generic interpretation of central-Northern states as straightforward “drought hotspots” is not retained.
