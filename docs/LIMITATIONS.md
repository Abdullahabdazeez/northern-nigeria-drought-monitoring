# Limitations

- MODIS, CHIRPS and ERA5-Land operate at different spatial resolutions.
- State-level summaries can conceal substantial variation within LGAs and agricultural zones.
- SPI results depend on the selected accumulation periods and climatological baseline.
- Vegetation indices respond to rainfall, temperature, land use, soil conditions, fire, grazing and cultivation; statistical association does not establish causation.
- Integrated pressure and hotspot scores are project-specific planning diagnostics, not standardised drought indices.
- Satellite-based vegetation condition does not directly measure crop yield or household vulnerability.
