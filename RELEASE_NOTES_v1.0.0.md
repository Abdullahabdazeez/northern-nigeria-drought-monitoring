## First prepared release

This repository presents a multi-source assessment of drought exposure, vegetation condition and long-term climate–vegetation dynamics across 19 states of Northern Nigeria.

### Included

- MODIS NDVI and EVI time-series analysis
- CHIRPS rainfall anomalies and SPI-3/SPI-6 drought indicators
- ERA5-Land growing-season temperature analysis
- Vegetation anomalies and Vegetation Condition Index
- Mann–Kendall trend tests and Sen's slope estimation
- Compound drought–vegetation hotspot assessment
- Climate–vegetation trend-decoupling analysis
- Integrated state and geopolitical-zone pressure rankings
- Six final maps and six analytical charts
- Selected GIS layers, time-series datasets and result tables
- Reproduction and repository-validation scripts
- Methodology, limitations and interpretation documentation

### Principal findings

- Regional growing-season NDVI declined significantly at −0.000777 per year.
- Regional EVI declined significantly at −0.000358 per year.
- Growing-season temperature increased significantly at +0.0154 °C per year.
- May–October rainfall increased by +2.50 mm per year, but the trend was not statistically significant.
- Ten of 19 states experienced significant NDVI browning.
- Nine states experienced significant warming.
- 2015 was the most spatially extensive compound drought–vegetation stress year, affecting 12 states.
- Nasarawa ranked highest in integrated climate–vegetation pressure.
- Kaduna ranked highest in compound drought–vegetation hotspot intensity.
